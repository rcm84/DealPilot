Document Type: Internal Sales Playbook
Company Name: DealPilot (Internal)
Contact Person: Emily Ross
Role: VP Sales
Version: 2026 Q2

Purpose:
Provide a repeatable framework for qualifying, advancing, and closing AI sales copilot opportunities in B2B SaaS accounts.

Ideal Customer Profile (ICP):
- SaaS companies with 50-500 quota-carrying reps.
- Existing CRM + conversation intelligence stack (Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong, or Chorus).
- Leadership mandate to improve forecast accuracy and rep productivity.

Common Buyer Roles:
1) RevOps Leader (process owner)
2) CRO / VP Sales (economic buyer)
3) Sales Enablement (adoption owner)
4) Salesforce Admin / IT Security (technical validators)

Top Pain Points to Diagnose:
- Incomplete opportunity hygiene and weak next-step discipline.
- Low manager visibility into deal execution quality.
- Long onboarding time for new reps.
- Forecast calls driven by anecdotes instead of evidence.

Core Opportunity Themes:
1) Productivity Gain: reduce manual note-taking and CRM updates.
2) Forecast Confidence: surface at-risk deals earlier.
3) Coaching at Scale: prioritize manager intervention opportunities.
4) Methodology Enforcement: identify qualification gaps automatically.

Discovery Questions:
- Which forecast KPI is currently least trusted?
- Where do reps lose the most time in post-call workflows?
- How is deal risk defined today, and who owns that definition?
- What would make a pilot unquestionably successful after 30 days?

Pilot Design Template:
- Cohort: 20-50 users across two teams.
- Duration: 30 days.
- Integrations: CRM + call recording platform.
- Weekly cadence: adoption review, quality review, executive readout.

Standard Next Steps for Qualified Deals:
1) Confirm success metrics and baseline values.
2) Finalize technical validation scope.
3) Run security review and legal checklist.
4) Present pilot-to-production rollout plan with ROI estimate.
