Call Objectives:
- Validate whether DealPilot can improve forecast confidence.
- Review integration path with Salesforce and Gong.
- Understand rollout model for a 200-rep distributed team.

Summary of Discussion:
FinServe has strong top-of-funnel volume but suffers from late-stage slippage and inconsistent qualification discipline. Alina wants a system that helps managers intervene earlier in at-risk opportunities. Jordan emphasized the need for concise, searchable summaries that map directly to existing sales methodology checkpoints.

Pain Points:
1) Forecast category changes happen late, often during final weekly call.
2) Managers cannot audit enough calls to coach reps effectively.
3) Reps duplicate effort across Gong notes, Salesforce updates, and Slack recaps.
4) Leadership lacks a single source of truth for deal progression signals.

Opportunities Identified:
1) Unified account timeline that consolidates calls, emails, and CRM updates.
2) Auto-detection of missing decision criteria and weak champion signals.
3) Manager dashboard for coaching queue prioritized by deal risk.
4) AI-assisted recap emails and mutual action plan generation.

Technical/Procurement Notes:
- FinServe requires SSO (Okta) and audit logging.
- Data retention must be configurable by region.
- Security team requested SOC 2 report and architecture diagram.

Agreed Next Steps:
1) DealPilot to provide sandbox with Salesforce + Gong connectors enabled.
2) FinServe to share 10 anonymized call transcripts for baseline scoring.
3) Joint success plan to define KPI targets for pilot by 2026-04-10.
4) Schedule executive checkpoint with CRO in week of 2026-04-14.
